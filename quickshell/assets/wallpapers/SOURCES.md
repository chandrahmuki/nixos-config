# Theme wallpaper sources

The selector retains the three existing Muggy/GNOME wallpapers from the repository.
For Quattro themes, original wallpapers are restored from the pinned Omarchy
Quattro source. Only the three source images whose filenames explicitly contain
`omarchy` (`2-omarchy.webp`, `06-omarchy.webp`, and `3-omarchy-plants.webp`)
are replaced with Muggynix branded wallpapers.

The three branded variants are generated by
`../../../scripts/generate-muggynix-wallpapers.sh`; other Quattro profile images
are restored from their original theme backgrounds and encoded as JPEG under
`quattro-<theme>.jpg` for renderer compatibility.
